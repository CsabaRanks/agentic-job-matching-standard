Agentic Job Matching Standard (AJMS)

Community Draft v0.1 — a proposal, not an adopted standard.

Maintain your candidate profile once. Share it selectively. Enable agents to compare opportunities in both directions.

This site hosts the published identifiers of AJMS v0.1:

  • JSON Schemas (Draft 2020-12): candidate-profile, job-profile, common, profile-reference, match-policy, match-result
  • JSON-LD context: context/v0.1/ajms.context.jsonld
  • Vocabulary (namespace ajms:): ns/v0.1/
